1. Washington State Supreme Court (5 Contested Seats)

Because Washington Judicial Conduct Canon 4 prohibits judicial candidates from promising rulings on future cases, candidate statements in the Voters' Pamphlet rely on coded signals. We evaluate candidates across five critical axes for selecting a state Supreme Court justice:

  • Appellate & Constitutional Mastery (1–10): The Supreme Court hears only legal appeals and constitutional questions (no jury trials or witnesses). How much direct experience does the candidate have drafting appellate opinions or arguing complex constitutional appeals?
  • Trial & Courtroom Breadth (1–10): Have they presided over or litigated across the diverse subject matters the Court reviews (civil, criminal, administrative, domestic, dependency)?
  • Judicial Philosophy Axis (1 = Textualist / "Apply As Written" ↔ 10 = Living Constitution / Systemic Equity): Lower scores signal strict textualism skeptical of recent Court precedents (e.g. State v. Blake or the capital gains tax). Higher scores signal alignment with the current Court majority's civil rights and equity focus.
  • Nonpartisan Restraint (1–10): Does the candidate maintain ethical judicial nonpartisanship, or do they indulge in partisan campaign attacks or ideological rhetoric?
  • Peer Consensus & Ratings (1–10): Ratings from independent bar associations ("Exceptionally Well Qualified") and endorsements from colleagues across all levels of the judiciary.

Initiative Measure No. IP26-645 (Initiative 645) Statewide Initiative to the People • State Pamphlet pp. 9–14, 64–65
Vote "YES" (Approve Initiative)
Repeal High-Earner Tax & Ban Future Income Taxes
Yes / Approve
What a "YES" Vote Does (Pamphlet Summary)

Repeals the 9.9% tax on annual individual/household income over $1,000,000 enacted by the Legislature under ESSB 6346 (scheduled to begin in 2028). Prohibits both state and local governments from imposing any tax on or measured by personal income, while leaving the bill's sales tax exemptions and B&O credits in place.

Vote "YES" (Approve Initiative)
Restrict Girls' Teams & Require Medical Sex Verification

Prohibits students defined as "biologically male" from competing on K-12 public or WIAA-affiliated private school sports teams designated for female students only. Requires every student competing in female-designated sports to submit a physician-signed form verifying biological sex based on "reproductive anatomy, genetic makeup, or normal endogenously produced testosterone levels."

Birth Certificates Disallowed by Text

Although the Pro rebuttal (p. 22) claims a doctor can verify sex "based on the student's birth records," the actual legal text in Section 3(2) (p. 69) states the provider may rely "only on one or more of the following: The student's reproductive anatomy, genetic makeup, or normal endogenously produced testosterone levels."
